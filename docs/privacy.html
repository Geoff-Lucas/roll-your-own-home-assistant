Home Organizer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Home Organizer is a self-hosted household kiosk: a touchscreen in one home that shows the family calendar, recipes, meal plans, timers and the weather. It is run privately by one household for its own members. It is not a public service, it has no user accounts of its own, and it doesn't sell, rent or share anyone's data. Its source code is public at github.com/Geoff-Lucas/roll-your-own-home-assistant.

Google Calendar data

When a household member signs in with Google, Home Organizer asks for access to their Google Calendar (and their email address, to find the right calendar). It uses that access only to:

  • show that person's calendar events on the kiosk screen, and
  • add, change or delete events when they do so from the kiosk.

Events are copied to the kiosk itself so the screen works without waiting on the internet. That copy stays on the kiosk and the household's own backup drive, and is refreshed from Google every few minutes. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on the kiosk. Calendar data is never sent to any other company or service, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.

Home Organizer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Voice commands

Speech is turned into text on the kiosk itself; no audio is recorded or sent anywhere. Everyday commands (timers, the time, the weather, restarting the kiosk) are handled on the kiosk. For a question it can't answer itself, the kiosk may send the text of that question to Anthropic's Claude service, along with the local time, the town name and the current weather, to get an answer. Anthropic may run a web search to answer it. No calendar data, recipes or sign-in details are ever included.

Other information

  • Recipes and meal plans are kept on the kiosk. Importing a recipe from a web address fetches that page.
  • Weather is looked up for the household's town from a public weather service.
  • Backups of the kiosk's data go only to a storage drive inside the same home.

Removing access and data

Anyone who linked their Google account can remove Home Organizer's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The kiosk then stops syncing, and removing the account from the kiosk deletes its copied events and its stored sign-in.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.
